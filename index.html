The Antidote to Subscriptions

In an era where wealth management tools are shifting towards costly monthly SaaS subscriptions and intrusive data-harvesting, BlueChip takes a radically different approach. We believe that tracking your hard-earned net worth shouldn't cost you a percentage of it. BlueChip is a sovereign, privacy-first tool that is entirely yours and completely free.

Intentional Investing

BlueChip fetches real-time stock prices to keep your valuation accurate, but puts you in control of the rest. Why? Because we are long-term investors. Corporate fundamentals and business exposures don't change by the minute. Manually logging your core data forces you to filter out the daily market noise and stay intensely focused on your long-term thesis.

The Core Engine

Tools built for absolute control.

BYOK Model (Bring Your Own Key)

AI Copilot Insights

Leverage institutional-grade quantitative analysis directly on your portfolio. Simply provide your own Google Gemini API key to unlock a context-aware financial conversational agent across every page of the app. Your data is sent securely and ephemerally to the LLM, with no middlemen collecting your prompts.

  • Instantly evaluates geographic risk, valuation multiples, and dividend sustainability.
  • Interact with your historical transaction data in a localized, secure chat environment.

Uncompromising Security

Your net worth is strictly private. Lock the application on startup via Touch ID or your macOS password. Activate Privacy Mode (⌘ + K) to instantly blur all sensitive figures.

Spotlight Global Search

Press ⌘ + F to invoke the highly optimized global search engine. Instantly navigate to specific modules, locate past transactions, or display the details of a tracked stock at the speed of thought.

Advanced Custom Settings

Designed for the international investor. Define your base currency (USD, EUR, GBP, CHF) and set your local dividend tax rate. BlueChip automatically calculates live FX rates to consolidate your global wealth.

Total Data Sovereignty

You are never trapped. Migrate seamlessly thanks to powerful CSV import/export capabilities for your positions and transactions. Export scheduled events in `.ical` format to sync directly with Apple Calendar.

Apple Silicon Optimized

The elegance and raw power of a true native application.

  • Lightning Fast Performance

    120 fps scrolling, instant rendering of complex charts, and a negligible memory footprint.

  • SwiftData Architecture
