## 📜 The Manifesto: The Sovereign Investor

In an era where wealth management tools are shifting towards costly monthly SaaS subscriptions and intrusive data-harvesting, **BlueChip** takes a radically different approach. 

We believe that tracking your hard-earned net worth shouldn't cost you a percentage of it. BlueChip is an uncompromising, privacy-first desktop application that is entirely yours and completely free. 

While the software fetches real-time market pricing automatically, it deliberately forces you to manually log your core fundamentals and theses. Why? Because we are long-term investors. Manually managing your core data forces you to filter out the daily market noise, avoid the trap of passive monitoring, and stay intensely focused on your long-term compounding strategy.

**Zero monthly subscriptions. Zero cloud data harvesting. Just cutting-edge quantitative analytics.**

## 🚀 The Core Engine

BlueChip is not a web wrapper or an Electron app. It is built natively from scratch using Apple's latest frameworks to fully harness the power of Apple Silicon.

*   ⚡️ **Lightning Fast Performance:** 120 fps scrolling, instant rendering of complex financial charts, and a negligible memory footprint.
*   🔒 **Uncompromising Security:** Your financial data never leaves your Mac. It is securely structured and locally encrypted directly on your SSD using **SwiftData**. Lock the app via **Touch ID**, and use Privacy Mode (⌘+K) to instantly blur sensitive figures.
*   🔄 **Live Market Sync:** Seamlessly connects to Yahoo Finance APIs to fetch live stock prices and real-time FX exchange rates.
*   🧠 **AI Copilot (BYOK):** A localized, context-aware financial conversational agent. Bring Your Own Key (Gemini) to perform institutional-grade analysis on your portfolio without middlemen collecting your prompts.
*   🔎 **Spotlight Integration:** Press ⌘+F to invoke a highly optimized global search engine spanning across your entire database (transactions, watchlist, thesis logs).
*   📊 **Menu Bar Companion:** Keep an eye on your wealth without breaking focus. A native macOS status bar widget provides instant access to daily performance and dividend tracking.

## 🧩 The 14 Analytical Modules

BlueChip provides an exhaustive suite of tools, replacing multiple premium web services into a single, cohesive interface.

### 1. Composition
The central command center. A holistic snapshot of your overall invested capital, available cash, and total unrealized returns, backed by 12 institutional-grade diagnostic charts (Geographic allocation, Asset Correlation heatmap, Return on Investment scatter plots).

### 2. Fundamentals
A powerful, fully customizable stock quality screener. Build your own scoring engine, define premium/standard thresholds, and rank your portfolio based on the financial metrics that matter most to your specific strategy.

### 3. Growth
The wealth accumulator engine. Isolates out-of-pocket deposits from pure market returns to reveal your true compounding trajectory. Tracks advanced metrics like CAGR, MoIC (Multiple on Invested Capital), and the "Tipping Point" where market returns overtake your active savings effort.

### 4. Dividends
The passive income snowball tracker. Bridges the gap between theoretical projected yields and actual cash collected. Features a 12-month forward-looking payout radar, YoY growth tracking, and Yield on Cost (YOC) expansion analytics.

### 5. Valuation
The intrinsic value desk. Evaluates your holdings through strict fundamental pricing models, historical multiples (10Y P/E), and cash flow yields. Enforce a global *Margin of Safety* to identify deep value opportunities and avoid multiple contraction risks.

### 6. Projections
A financial time machine. Uses your historical portfolio data and advanced compounding algorithms to forecast your future net worth over a 50-year horizon, adjusting for DRIP (Dividend Reinvestment) and inflation.

### 7. Simulation
The ultimate risk-free sandbox. An isolated clone of your portfolio allowing you to test theoretical trades, rebalancing strategies, and cash deployments (complete with tax/fee drag calculators) without altering your real accounting data.

### 8. Watchlist
The prospective command center. Monitor potential investments, evaluate their intrinsic value, and strictly compare their fundamental footprint against the assets you already own via the Quality Analysis Laboratory.

### 9. Exposure
The macroeconomic risk manager. Dives deep into actual geographic revenue streams (USA vs Rest of World) to reveal your true geopolitical footprint, foreign exchange (FX) risks, and dangerous Sector × Geography concentrations.

### 10. Transactions
A complete, chronological audit trail (Ledger) of every deposit, withdrawal, buy, and sell order. Analyzes behavioral metrics, holding periods, and calculates your precise Tax & Fee Drag ratio.

### 11. Benchmark
The ultimate Active vs. Passive test. Measures your true Alpha by comparing your portfolio's annual performance against major custom indices (e.g., S&P 500, NASDAQ-100) and visualizes the absolute opportunity cost via a 10k simulated growth chart.

### 12. Calendar
A 12-month rolling event horizon. Automatically tracks US market holidays, purchase anniversaries, and quadruple witching days. Allows manual scheduling of earnings calls and macro catalysts with a built-in `.ical` export feature.

### 13. Wealth
The Master Balance Sheet & FIRE Planner. Integrates your brokerage account with external, manually added assets (Real Estate, Crypto) and liabilities (Mortgages, Loans). Calculates your True Net Worth, Debt-to-Asset ratio, and estimates passive income based on your Safe Withdrawal Rate (SWR).

### 14. Thesis
The Institutional Analyst Desk. Replaces emotional decision-making with rigorous qualitative research. Documents business anatomy, structural moats, bear case threats, and exit strategies. Features a *Global Event Stream* to log catalyst updates and track the evolution of your conviction status over time.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

*   **Platform:** macOS 14.0+ (Optimized for Apple Silicon)
*   **UI Framework:** SwiftUI
*   **Database:** SwiftData (Persistent local storage, completely offline)
*   **State Management:** Combine / `@ObservableObject`
*   **Data APIs:** Yahoo Finance (Real-time pricing & FX)
*   **AI Integration:** Google Gemini API (BYOK architecture)
*   **Charts:** Apple Swift Charts framework
